We accept all major Australian health funds including BUPA, Medibank, HCF, NIB, and CBHS. We have HICAPS on-site so your rebate is processed instantly at the time of your visit — no paperwork or delays.
The easiest way is to use our online booking form below — choose your preferred date and service and we'll confirm within 2 hours during business hours. You can also call us on 07 4600 0000 or send an email to hello@smilebrightdental.com.au.
We'll take a full set of digital X-rays and photos, carry out a comprehensive oral health exam, and discuss any concerns you have. At the end, we'll walk you through exactly what we found and give you a written treatment plan with costs before anything is decided.
Yes! We offer Afterpay for treatments up to $2,000, and we can discuss interest-free payment plans for larger treatments like implants or orthodontics. We believe cost should never be a barrier to good dental health.
Most adults benefit from a check-up and clean every 6 months. Children should be seen every 6 months too. If you have gum disease, a history of cavities, or wear braces, we may recommend more frequent visits — we'll advise you at your appointment.
Professional teeth whitening performed or supervised by a dentist is very safe. We use clinically tested, TGA-approved whitening gels at concentrations that are highly effective without damaging your enamel. Over-the-counter products from pharmacies are much weaker and often less predictable.
